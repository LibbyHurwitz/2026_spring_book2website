Kakuzo Okakura

Kakuzo Okakura was a Japanese scholar, curator, and philosopher who played a pivotal role in the preservation of traditional Japanese art and culture during the Meiji era — a period of rapid westernization.

Educated in both Japanese and Western traditions, he served at the Tokyo National Museum and later became a curator at the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston. The Book of Tea, published in 1906, remains his most celebrated work — a meditation on tea as a vehicle for philosophy, beauty, and the art of living.
